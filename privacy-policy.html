Privacy policy.

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This policy explains what information Guardian Digital Studios, L.L.C. ("we") collects when you use our websites, apps and games, and how we use it.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly, such as your email address when you contact us. Our apps may store the data you enter (for example, deck lists or game logs) on your device. Where an app offers optional sync or accounts, that data is stored on our servers only after you opt in.

How we use it

We use your information to operate and improve our products, respond to your messages and keep our services secure. We do not sell your personal information.

Analytics and third parties

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand how our products are used. App stores and payment providers process purchases under their own policies.

Your choices

You can delete data stored in our apps at any time from within the app or by uninstalling it. To request access to or deletion of information we hold about you, email us.

Children

Our products are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.

Questions about this policy: email us.
