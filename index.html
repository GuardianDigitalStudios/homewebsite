About Us

Independent
by design

Guardian Digital Studios is an independent software studio based in Kensington, Maryland. We design and build applications for people who play games — the tools that sit alongside a hobby and carry the parts of it worth keeping track of.

Our work starts from experience. Every product begins with a problem we have run into ourselves, so we know what the software has to get right before a line of it is written. That focus is deliberate: our applications do one job thoroughly rather than several of them loosely.

Commander Index is our flagship release, built for Magic: The Gathering Commander playgroups and free to everyone who uses it. It sets the standard we hold the rest of our work to.

🎮

Built from experience

Commander Index grew out of our own playgroup. We build the software we rely on, which is why it holds up in use.

Free, in full
