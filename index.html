About Us

A small studio
in Maryland

Guardian Digital Studios is a small team in Kensington, Maryland. We build apps for the games we play ourselves, which is most of the reason any of them get finished.

We started because the software around our hobbies kept getting in the way. Some of it is priced for people with expense accounts. A lot of it is stuffed with features nobody asked for, and slow because of them.

So we keep things small. One app that does its job beats four that almost do. Everything we've shipped is free to use, and Commander Index is the first one out the door.

🎮

We use what we build

Commander Index exists because our own pod needed it and nothing out there fit.

💰

Free by default

Every feature in Commander Index works without paying. Supporters turn off the ads and get some cosmetics. That's the whole business model.

⚙️

Small on purpose

We'd rather finish one app properly than keep four of them in permanent beta.

MOBILE APP

Commander Index

An app for Magic: The Gathering Commander playgroups. Score how strong a deck is, log your games so the rest of the table can confirm them, keep life totals during play, and put a number on the "is that deck too strong for this table" argument.
