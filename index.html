Guardian Digital Studios is an independent studio in Kensington, Maryland. Today that means Commander Index, a companion app for Magic: The Gathering playgroups, coming soon to iOS and Android. A game of our own is in early development.

A studio in
Kensington

We build companion software for games people already play, and we are making a game of our own.

Commander Index is the first: an app for Magic: The Gathering Commander playgroups that scores how strong a deck is, records who won, and keeps the table's life totals. It is coming to iOS and Android.

01

Works without an account

The game tracker keeps life totals, commander damage and poison for up to six players with no sign-up.

02

No paid features

Every feature is free. Supporters remove the ads and unlock cosmetic extras: themes, colours and a badge. Nothing functional sits behind a payment.

03

No tracking

Our ads are not personalised. Nothing follows you between apps, and nothing about the decks you build or the games you play is used to choose what you are shown.

04

Contact

Support and general enquiries go to hello@guardiandigitalstudios.com. Messages are read and answered.

What we make

Commander Index

The companion app for Magic: The Gathering Commander playgroups. Score deck power across twelve weighted categories, log matches the whole table confirms, track pod statistics, and keep life totals through a full game.
