Responsive behaviour

Three breakpoints today. Content-driven rather than device-driven, and worth re-deriving rather than inheriting.

BreakpointWhat changes
≤ 900pxProduct card drops to one column and its image panel swaps its right border for a bottom one; footer grid goes to two columns with the brand block spanning both
≤ 680pxNav collapses to the hamburger; value cards and footer grid stack to one column; hero drops its 660px floor; type sizes step down
≤ 400pxWordmark shrinks a step; product card padding tightens
Services needs no breakpoint, and that is the pattern to prefer.services is repeat(auto-fit,minmax(min(240px,100%),1fr)), so it drops 3 → 2 → 1 on available space alone. The min(240px,100%) is load-bearing: a bare minmax(240px,1fr) cannot shrink below its track and overflows a narrow viewport. Prefer this over adding a fourth breakpoint.
The hero is not full-viewport-heightmin-height:660px, dropping to 0 at ≤680px where the padding alone sets the height. An earlier version of this page claimed 100vh with a 100svh fallback; neither appears in the stylesheet. A fixed floor was the point, it does not push everything below the fold on a short landscape screen the way a viewport unit would.
Reading measureThe legal pages are the only long-form reading on the site and need a comfortable line length at every width. Body copy elsewhere is capped around 320–600px depending on the block.
Test the footer at narrow widths.fgrid is three columns, 1.6fr 1fr 1fr (brand, navigation, legal), not four. It reflows twice, to two columns at 900px with the brand spanning both, then to one at 680px. It is also the region where every contrast failure on this site has happened.
