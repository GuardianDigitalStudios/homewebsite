Responsive behaviour

Three breakpoints today. Content-driven rather than device-driven, and worth re-deriving rather than inheriting.

BreakpointWhat changes
≤ 900pxProducts grid drops to one column
≤ 680pxNav collapses to the hamburger; About grid and value cards stack; type sizes step down
≤ 400pxSmall-phone adjustments
The hero is full-viewport-heightmin-height: 100vh with a 100svh fallback for mobile browser chrome. Not a requirement, it is a choice, and on a short landscape screen it pushes everything below the fold.
Reading measureThe legal pages are the only long-form reading on the site and need a comfortable line length at every width. Body copy elsewhere is capped around 320–600px depending on the block.
Test the footer at narrow widthsIt is a four-column layout that has to reflow, and it is also the region where every contrast failure has happened.
