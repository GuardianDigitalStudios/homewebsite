Pages and section order

Three pages. Section order as it stands today, the order is not sacred, the content is.

index.html

SectionJob
NavHome / About / Services / Products, sticky, logo left
HeroSay what the studio does and on what terms. It no longer names the product
AboutTwo paragraphs plus four value cards. Paragraph one names the Services / Products split the rest of the page depends on
ServicesThree service cards plus the enquiry CTA. Client work
ProductsOne product card (Commander Index). Our own work
FooterBrand blurb, navigation, legal, copyright
Services and Products are adjacent and easy to conflateServices is what we will build for you; Products is what we have built for ourselves. Products doubles as the only evidence behind the Services claims, which is why it sits directly after rather than before.
