The button label must not be the addressIt says "Email us about a project" and not hello@guardiandigitalstudios.com on purpose. The address is 240px of text with no break opportunity; inside a pill with 44px of horizontal padding it needs 284px, and with .page's 48px it needs 332px, so a bare-address label scrolls the page sideways under 332px. Several short words wrap at a space and set no floor. Same failure as the card 04 min-content trap in value cards.

overflow-wrap:anywhere on .cta covers the address where it appears as running text in the body line.

Why mailto and not a formThe site is static on Cloudflare Pages with no backend, and the CSP sets form-action 'self'. A real enquiry form needs a Pages Function or Worker to receive the POST plus spam handling, which is a backend in a repo that deliberately has none. mailto: costs nothing and works today. If a form is ever wanted, that is the trade being made.
No breakpoint of its own.services is repeat(auto-fit,minmax(min(240px,100%),1fr)), so it drops 3 → 2 → 1 on available space alone. The min(240px,100%) is load-bearing: a bare minmax(240px,1fr) cannot shrink below 240px and overflows on a narrow viewport.
