The headline sells the engagement model, not the outputFour verbs and a qualifier. The verbs name the whole process; "any part of it" is the actual claim, which is that a client does not have to hand over a whole project from zero to be worth talking to. The sub is what makes it concrete, three entry points: from scratch, mid-build, or already live.

This is why the headline no longer names a product. Commander Index is named in About and again in Products, which is twice already.

HistoryTwo rewrites, each because the headline claimed something untrue. It was "Apps for people who play games" while the studio described itself as making games; it does not make games. It then became "We make apps, and the websites that sit beside them", which framed websites as companions to our own apps at the point where the site started offering to build one for a client. Do not reintroduce a headline that describes only our own products.
There is still no call to action here, and that is now worth arguing aboutIt used to be defensible on its own terms: the studio sold nothing directly, and the only action on the site was the outbound product link. That reason is gone. The headline now makes a commercial claim and the nearest way to act on it is the Services CTA, one section down. A hero that pitches for work with no way to respond is a different decision from a hero that pitches nothing.
